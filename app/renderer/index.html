    What to try during a session
    1. Baseline: let startup and the library scan finish; browse the library normally.
    2. Transport: play, pause, resume, seek, previous, next, and let one track end on its own.
    3. Volume: sweep slowly and quickly, make small changes, mute/unmute, and change volume while paused and playing.
    4. Scrubber: click-seek, short drag, long drag and repeated drags.
    5. Queue: reorder and select tracks, and play through several transitions.
    6. Persistence: check volume/shuffle/repeat/queue/position, restart Hive and confirm playback restores paused.
    7. Stress: scroll large views, switch tabs, and open artwork and the queue while audio plays.

    LyricsEmbedded lyrics are preferred.
    This tab always shows the plain embedded lyrics -- the ones saved to the audio file. Automatic search follows Settings → Highlighted Lyrics: when enabled, LRCLIB is preferred and the plain text is embedded here; when disabled, Hive uses Genius for plain lyrics. The player's synced/highlighted display during playback is separate and never affects what's embedded.
    Saving writes the lyrics to the audio file. Automatic search alone does not overwrite embedded lyrics.
